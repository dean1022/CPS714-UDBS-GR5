THE PEOPLE BEHIND UDBS

About Us

We're Group 5 from Toronto Metropolitan University. We developed the University Digital Borrowing System (UDBS) as our group project for CPS714: Software Project Management.

What we made

UDBS is a prototype digital library for university students and faculty. It lets users sign in, explore and search electronic literature, check availability, borrow titles and manage their active loans in a Personal Library. Our goal was to build a clear, usable borrowing experience while practising software development, sprint planning, integration and testing as a team.
